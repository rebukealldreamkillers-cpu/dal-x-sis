What this sample measures

This is a curated set, not a count of how often AI systems act without authority. An incident appears here only when a public source documents an executed action, the system involved, and what followed. Most organizations have no duty to disclose agent incidents, so the public record holds a small share of them. The figures below show the scale outside this sample.

Trackers and surveys use their own methods, and several are run by security vendors. They indicate scale; they are not a verified count.
